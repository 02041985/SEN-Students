Tier 3 teaching moves

  1. Concrete, then picture, then number. Do each task with real objects (coins, counters, a real clock) before the screen version.
  2. One skill at a time. Stay at 2 answer buttons until the student gets 4 out of 5 right first time on two different days.
  3. Errorless first. Keep "show the answer" on for new skills, then switch it off to check mastery.
  4. Short and often. Three 5-minute rounds across the day beat one long lesson.
  5. Respect their age. Use real-life contexts (money, time, signs) and avoid toddler-style content where possible. The library marks which resources look young.
  6. Talk the same way every time. Use the same words for the same idea ("take away", "how many altogether") across staff and home.
